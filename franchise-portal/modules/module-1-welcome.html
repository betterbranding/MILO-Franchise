Our Mission

MILO Insulation was founded with a simple mission: make every home more comfortable, energy-efficient, and safe — using the power of nature. We specialize in one revolutionary product:

🌾

MILEX Sorghum-Based Insulation

A loose-fill, blown-in insulation made from American-grown grain sorghum (milo). Plant-based, renewable, non-toxic, fire-resistant (self-extinguishing), pest-resistant, and mold-resistant. Naturally better insulation for attics, walls, crawl spaces, metal buildings, new construction, and retrofits.

🌍 Our Sustainability Story

MILEX is made from grain sorghum — a drought-tolerant crop that uses less water than alternatives, grown right here in Texas by American farmers. By choosing MILO, your customers are choosing a truly sustainable, renewable insulation that supports American agriculture and reduces environmental impact. "Naturally Better Insulation."

MILO says: "Welcome aboard, partner! You're joining a movement — not just a franchise. MILEX insulation is something truly different: safe for families, safe for the planet, and made right here in America from grain sorghum. I'm excited to guide you through every step!"

What to Expect During Onboarding

Your onboarding is organized into 9 sequential modules. Complete each module to unlock the next. Here's an overview:

  1. Welcome to MILO — You are here! Get oriented with the brand and your portal.
  2. Legal & Business Setup — Form your business entity, secure insurance, and handle licensing.
  3. Contacts & Vendors — Meet your key suppliers and set up vendor accounts.
  4. Equipment Ordering — Order your sales/inspection gear and production equipment.
  5. Production Training — Learn MILEX sorghum-based insulation, processing techniques, and safety protocols.
  6. Sales Process — Master home inspections, quoting, and closing deals.
  7. CRM & Operations — Set up JobNimbus and learn your day-to-day operational tools.
  8. Marketing Setup — Build your local brand presence and marketing engine.
  9. Launch Checklist — Final checks, 30/60/90-day goals, and your grand opening plan.

⏱️ Timeline Expectations

Target completion: 2–4 weeks. Here's a suggested pace:

Week 1 Modules 1–3
Week 2 Modules 4–5
Week 3 Modules 6–7
