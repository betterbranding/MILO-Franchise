📦 MILEX Insulation Product Overview

MILEX is MILO's proprietary sorghum-based, loose-fill, blown-in insulation — made from American-grown grain sorghum (milo). It's a plant-based, renewable product engineered for superior thermal performance, fire resistance, and ease of application.

🌾 MILEX Sorghum-Based Blown-In Insulation

  • Plant-based & renewable — made from grain sorghum grown by American farmers
  • Non-toxic — safe for families, pets, and installers
  • Fire-resistant — self-extinguishing properties
  • Pest-resistant — naturally deters insects and rodents
  • Mold-resistant — won't support mold growth
  • Loose-fill blown-in application — ideal for attics, walls, crawl spaces, metal buildings
  • Processed with the MX-750 Extruder, installed with the MILEX Blower & Vacuum
  • Made in Texas from drought-tolerant, American-grown grain sorghum

🌍 Why Sorghum?

Grain sorghum is a drought-tolerant crop that uses significantly less water than alternatives. By choosing MILEX, customers support American agriculture and get a truly sustainable insulation. Competitors use fiberglass (itchy, settles, not fire-resistant), cellulose (treated with chemicals, can mold), or spray foam (toxic chemicals, expensive, requires heavy PPE). MILEX is "Naturally Better Insulation."

MILO says: "This is where it gets exciting! You'll learn how to process grain sorghum into MILEX insulation with the MX-750 Extruder, and then blow it into homes with the MILEX Blower. Two machines, one amazing product — and it's all plant-based and non-toxic. Let me show you the way! 👉"

🔥 Fire Test Station — Build & Testing

The fire test demonstration is one of your most powerful sales tools. Master this process to confidently show customers why MILEX sorghum-based insulation is the safest choice.

Build Procedure:

  1. Prepare the base — Secure a metal or concrete platform at a comfortable demonstration height. Ensure it's stable and on a non-flammable surface.
  2. Install the sample frame — Mount the sample holding frame to the base. It should securely hold insulation samples vertically.
  3. Set up flame source — Position butane torch holder at a consistent distance from the sample frame for repeatable results.
  4. Prepare sample materials — Cut MILEX sorghum-based insulation samples and competitor samples (fiberglass, cellulose) to uniform size (approximately 6" x 6").
  5. Safety station — Position fire extinguisher within arm's reach. Have a water bucket as backup.

Testing Procedure:

  1. Mount MILEX sample in the testing frame and ensure it's secure.
  2. Apply flame to sample using the butane torch for 10–15 seconds of direct contact.
  3. Remove flame and observe — MILEX will self-extinguish within seconds. The material chars but does not sustain combustion.
  4. Document results — Record photos and video for use in customer presentations and proposals.
  5. Repeat with competitor sample — Demonstrate how fiberglass melts and cellulose continues to burn, highlighting MILEX's superiority.
  6. Present findings — Use the results in your sales presentations to build trust and urgency.

🦺 Safety Protocols & PPE Requirements

✅ Good news — MILEX is non-toxic! Because MILEX is a plant-based, sorghum insulation product, it does not contain the harmful chemicals found in spray foam or chemically-treated cellulose. PPE requirements are simpler and focused on general job site safety.

Required PPE:

Dust mask / N95 respirator Protects against dust particles during blowing operations
Safety glasses / goggles Eye protection required on all job sites
Hearing protection Required when operating the MX-750 Extruder and MILEX Blower equipment
Work gloves General-purpose work gloves for handling equipment and materials
Steel-toe boots Foot protection on all job sites

🔧 Common Issues & Troubleshooting

Blower clogging or inconsistent feed

Check for moisture in MILEX material. Clear hose blockages. Verify material is properly processed and broken up before loading into the blower.

Inconsistent blown-in density

Adjust blower speed and air-to-material ratio. Maintain consistent hose distance from target area. Check for cavity obstructions that may redirect airflow.

MX-750 extrusion quality issues

Verify sorghum pellet quality and moisture content. Check extruder temperature settings. Inspect extrusion dies for wear or blockage. Clean and recalibrate as needed.

Insufficient coverage in tight spaces

Use extension hoses and smaller nozzle attachments. Adjust blower pressure for tight cavities. Consider fill-and-check approach for wall cavities.

Material settling after installation

Ensure proper density during installation. Overfill by 10-15% to account for minimal settling. Verify blower settings match recommended specifications.
