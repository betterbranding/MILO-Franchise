⚡ JobNimbus Setup & Login

JobNimbus is your all-in-one CRM and project management platform. It's where you'll manage leads, schedule jobs, create invoices, and track your entire business.

  1. Accept your invitation — Check your email for the JobNimbus team invitation from MILO corporate.
  2. Set up your profile — Add your name, photo, contact info, and territory details.
  3. Download the mobile app — Available on iOS and Android. Essential for field work.
  4. Configure notifications — Set up push notifications for new leads, appointment reminders, and task alerts.
  5. Review pre-built workflows — MILO has pre-configured your pipeline stages and templates. Familiarize yourself with each.
🔑 Login Issues? Contact the training team at training@miloinsulation.com if you need help accessing your JobNimbus account.

📊 Pipeline Stages

Every lead and job moves through these stages. Keep your pipeline updated in real-time for accurate reporting.

1

New Lead

Incoming inquiry — web form, phone call, or referral. Contact within 5 minutes.

2

Appointment Scheduled

Home inspection appointment booked. Send confirmation to homeowner.

3

Inspection Complete

Inspection done, findings documented, preparing proposal.

4

Proposal Sent

Quote/proposal delivered to homeowner. Follow-up schedule begins.

5

Contract Signed

Customer accepted. Deposit collected. Ready to schedule installation.

6

Job Scheduled

Installation date set. MILEX materials ordered. Crew assigned.

7

In Progress

Installation underway. Update status as work progresses.

8

Job Complete

Work finished. Final inspection passed. Photos documented.

9

Invoiced / Paid

Final invoice sent and payment collected. Request review.

📥 Lead Management Workflow

  1. Instant response — Respond to every new lead within 5 minutes. Speed-to-lead is the #1 factor in conversion.
  2. Qualify the lead — Confirm they're a homeowner in your territory. Ask about their insulation concerns.
  3. Book the appointment — Schedule the inspection. Aim for within 48 hours of first contact.
  4. Log everything — Record all communication, notes, and status changes in JobNimbus.
  5. Follow the pipeline — Move the contact through stages as you progress. Never leave a lead stuck.

📅 Job Scheduling & Calendar

Use the JobNimbus calendar to manage your schedule:

  • Block inspection times — Set regular windows for home inspections (e.g., mornings)
  • Schedule installations — Allow adequate time per job based on scope
  • Set travel buffers — Account for travel time between appointments
  • Sync with your phone — JobNimbus calendar syncs with Google/Apple calendars
  • Automated reminders — Customers receive appointment reminders automatically

💳 Invoicing & Payments

JobNimbus handles your invoicing and payment collection:

  • Create invoices from approved proposals with one click
  • Accept payments via credit card, ACH, and check
  • Collect deposits at contract signing (typically 50%)
  • Send final invoices upon job completion for remaining balance
  • Track payment status and send automated payment reminders
  • Export to accounting for QuickBooks/Xero integration

💬 Customer Communication Tracking

Log every customer interaction in JobNimbus to maintain a complete history:

  • Phone calls — log duration and key discussion points
  • Text messages — sent directly through JobNimbus
  • Emails — sync your email for automatic logging
  • In-person meetings — add notes after every appointment
  • Photos and documents — attach to the job record
