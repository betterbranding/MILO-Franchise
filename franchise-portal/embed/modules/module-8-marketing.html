📍 Google Business Profile Setup

Your Google Business Profile (GBP) is crucial for appearing in local search results when homeowners search for insulation services in your area.

Create or claim your Google Business Profile Go to business.google.com and set up your listing for "MILO Insulation of [Your City]"
Complete all profile fields Business name, address, phone, hours, service area, website URL
Add service categories Primary: "Insulation Contractor" — Additional: "Energy Auditor," "Home Improvement"
Write business description Highlight MILEX sorghum-based insulation, eco-friendly, plant-based, non-toxic, fire-resistant, blown-in insulation, energy audits, attic insulation
Upload photos Add your team photos, MILO branding, job site before/after photos, and MILEX product photos
Verify your listing Complete Google's verification process (postcard, phone, or video verification)

🔍 Local SEO Strategy

Optimize your online presence for local search to generate organic leads. Focus on these keywords and platforms:

🎯 Target Keywords

  • "MILEX sorghum-based insulation" — your unique product
  • "blown-in insulation [your city]"
  • "attic insulation [your city]"
  • "energy audit [your city]"
  • "eco-friendly insulation" — green messaging differentiator
  • "plant-based insulation" — unique to MILEX
  • "insulation contractor [your city]"
  • "wall insulation [your city]"
🌿 Eco-Friendly Messaging — Your Differentiator: MILEX's plant-based, sorghum story is a massive marketing advantage. Lean into sustainability messaging: "Naturally Better Insulation," made from American-grown sorghum, supports local farmers, drought-tolerant crop, renewable resource. This resonates strongly with eco-conscious homeowners and sets you apart from every competitor.
MILO says: "☝️ Marketing is where you tell the MILO story! Remember — no other insulation company can say their product is made from American-grown grain sorghum. That's YOUR superpower. Lead with the eco-friendly message, show off those fire test videos, and watch the leads pour in!"

⭐ Review Generation Strategy

Online reviews are the #1 factor in local search rankings and customer trust. Build your review count from day one.

Create a review request process Ask every completed customer for a Google review within 24 hours of job completion
Set up review link shortcut Create a direct link to your Google review page for easy sharing via text
Respond to all reviews Reply to every review (positive and negative) within 24 hours
🎯 Goal: 5 reviews in your first month, 20+ by month 3. Reviews mentioning "eco-friendly," "plant-based," "fire-resistant," and "sorghum insulation" help your SEO enormously.
