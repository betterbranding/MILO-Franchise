🔥 Fire Test Station Build

The Fire Test Station is a demonstration tool used to showcase the fire-resistant properties of MILEX sorghum-based insulation to potential customers. It's a powerful sales tool that builds trust and differentiates MILO from competitors.

📋 Purpose: The fire test station allows you to demonstrate, in real time, that MILEX insulation is self-extinguishing — a major selling point for homeowners concerned about safety.

Components Needed:

Fire-safe demonstration base Metal or concrete base platform for safe flame testing
Sample mounting frame Frame to hold insulation samples during testing
Butane torch / controlled flame source Consistent, controllable flame for repeatable demonstrations
MILEX sorghum-based insulation samples Pre-cut samples for demonstration — order from Gemini Insulation Supply
Competitor insulation samples (for comparison) Source standard fiberglass and cellulose samples for side-by-side comparison
Fire extinguisher (safety) Always have a fire extinguisher on hand during demonstrations
📐 Build Diagram: Refer to the Fire Test Station build guide in your training documents for detailed assembly instructions and dimensions.

Testing Procedure Overview:

  1. Set up the fire test station on a safe, non-flammable surface
  2. Mount MILEX sorghum-based insulation sample in the testing frame
  3. Apply flame to sample using the butane torch for 10–15 seconds
  4. Observe self-extinguishing properties — MILEX will stop burning when flame is removed
  5. Document results — take photos/video for customer presentation
  6. Repeat with competitor samples (fiberglass, cellulose) to demonstrate the difference
  7. Present results to homeowner to reinforce safety benefits
