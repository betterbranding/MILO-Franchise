🏛️ LLC / Business Entity Formation

You'll need to form a legal business entity (typically an LLC) in your state before operating your franchise.

Choose your business name Must include "MILO Insulation" — e.g., "MILO Insulation of [City], LLC"
File LLC with your state File Articles of Organization with your state's Secretary of State
Obtain Operating Agreement Create an LLC Operating Agreement (consult your attorney)
Register for state/local business licenses Requirements vary by state and municipality
Obtain registered agent (if required) Some states require a registered agent for your LLC
MILO says: "I know legal paperwork isn't the most exciting part, but getting this foundation right is key to your success! Take it one step at a time — you've got this. 👍 And remember, our corporate team is here to help if you get stuck."

🛡️ Required Insurance

The following insurance policies are required before you begin operations. Contact your insurance agent to set up coverage.

General Liability Insurance Minimum $1M per occurrence / $2M aggregate — Covers property damage and bodily injury claims
Workers' Compensation Insurance Required in most states — Covers employee injuries on the job
Commercial Auto Insurance Covers your work vehicles — Required for any vehicle used for business
Professional Liability Insurance Also known as Errors & Omissions (E&O) — Covers claims related to your professional services
💡 Tip: Ask your insurance agent about a Business Owner's Policy (BOP) which may bundle several coverages at a lower cost.

📋 State & Local Licensing

Insulation contractors may need specific licenses depending on your state and locality. Research and obtain all required permits.

State contractor's license Check your state's contractor licensing board for insulation/general contractor requirements
County/city business license Contact your local government offices for municipal business license requirements
Home improvement contractor registration Required in some states — Check if your state requires HIC registration
EPA Lead-Safe certification (if applicable) Required when working on pre-1978 homes — Obtain EPA RRP certification

🏦 EIN & Business Banking

Apply for Employer Identification Number (EIN) Free from the IRS at irs.gov — Takes minutes online. You'll need this for banking, taxes, and hiring.
Open a business bank account Keep business and personal finances separate. Bring your EIN, LLC documents, and ID to your bank.
Set up a business credit card Recommended for tracking business expenses and building business credit
Set up accounting software QuickBooks, Xero, or similar — Track income, expenses, and prepare for taxes
⚠️ Important: All legal documents, insurance certificates, and licensing must be completed and submitted to corporate before you can proceed to equipment ordering and operations. This protects both you and the MILO brand.
