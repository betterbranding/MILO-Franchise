🏠 Home Inspection Walkthrough

The home inspection is your opportunity to build trust, demonstrate expertise, and identify insulation problems. Follow this process for every appointment:

  1. Arrive on time, in uniform — MILO branded shirt, hat, and clean appearance. First impressions matter.
  2. Introduction & rapport building — Introduce yourself, explain the inspection process, and ask about the homeowner's comfort concerns.
  3. Exterior walkthrough — Check exterior walls, foundation, soffits, and ventilation. Note any visible issues.
  4. Attic inspection — Access the attic with your ladder. Check existing insulation type, depth, and condition. Take thermal camera readings.
  5. Crawl space / basement check — Inspect rim joists, foundation walls, and floor insulation if applicable.
  6. Interior assessment — Check for drafts around windows, doors, and outlets. Use thermal camera to identify hot/cold spots.
  7. Document findings — Take photos and notes for every area inspected. Record measurements.
  8. Present findings to homeowner — Walk through your thermal images and photos, explaining what you found.

📷 Using Your Thermal Camera for Energy Audits

Your thermal imaging camera is your most powerful sales tool. It makes invisible energy loss visible to homeowners.

🔴

Hot Spots

Red/yellow areas indicate heat gain — missing or inadequate insulation

🔵

Cold Spots

Blue/purple areas indicate air infiltration and heat loss

🟢

Even Temps

Consistent green indicates properly insulated areas

💡 Pro Tip: Always show the homeowner the thermal images in real-time on your camera screen. The visual impact of seeing energy waste is the most effective sales tool you have.

💡 Identifying Problems & MILEX Selling Points

Common insulation problems to identify and communicate to homeowners:

  • Insufficient attic insulation — Most homes have less than the recommended R-value
  • Gaps and voids — Even small gaps dramatically reduce insulation effectiveness
  • Settled insulation — Older insulation settles over time, losing R-value
  • Air leaks — Unsealed penetrations, gaps around pipes, wiring, and ducts
  • Moisture issues — Condensation indicates air infiltration and insulation failure
  • Pest damage — Rodents and insects can destroy existing insulation
  • High energy bills — Direct correlation to insulation performance

💰 Quoting & Pricing

📋 Pricing Guidelines

Detailed pricing guidelines and rate cards will be provided during your training session. Pricing varies by region, project scope, and target R-value.

📄 Proposal Template

MILO proposal templates are available in your documents folder. Use these branded templates for every quote to maintain consistency.

📐 Key Pricing Factors: Square footage, MILEX insulation depth, R-value target, accessibility, existing insulation removal, and any special conditions (crawl spaces, metal buildings, etc.).

🤝 Closing Techniques

  1. Summarize findings — Restate the problems you identified and the impact on their home comfort and energy bills.
  2. Present the MILEX solution — Explain how MILEX sorghum-based insulation solves their problems: specific R-values, energy savings, and the unique benefits (non-toxic, fire-resistant, eco-friendly).
  3. Show the fire test results — If you performed a demo, remind them of MILEX's safety advantages vs. fiberglass and cellulose.
  4. Present the proposal — Walk through the quote line by line. Be transparent about scope and pricing.
  5. Address objections — Listen actively. Common objections: price, timing, "need to think about it." Have responses ready.
  6. Ask for the sale — "Would you like to go ahead and schedule the installation?" Be direct and confident.
  7. Handle paperwork — If they say yes, complete the contract on-site. Collect deposit via your payment system.
