Our Story

We Turned a Sorghum Seed Into the Insulation of the Future.

More than 30 years ago, MILO Insulation's parent company — MILEX Corporation — pioneered a patented process to extrude natural foam from sorghum grain. No harmful polymers. No synthetic fillers. Just nature's engineering, refined.

What began as a sustainable alternative to packing peanuts revealed something remarkable: MILEX™ is one of the most efficient natural insulators ever created. Long-lasting. Pest-resistant. Fire-resistant. Biodegradable.

Today, we manufacture, sell, and install MILEX™ insulation through a vertically integrated franchise model — and we're expanding nationally.
