How It Started

It Began With a Sorghum Seed and a Big Idea.

More than 30 years ago, our founders identified something remarkable: sorghum grain — an agricultural staple grown by American farmers — could be extruded into a natural, biodegradable foam through a patented, polymer-free process.

Under the MILEX Corporation banner, we developed this innovation into an environmentally responsible alternative to polystyrene packing peanuts. The market responded. The brand grew. And we kept pushing forward.

But the real breakthrough came when we discovered that MILEX™ wasn't just a packaging material — it was one of the most effective natural insulators on the market.

The Breakthrough

From Packing Peanuts to the Insulation of the Future.

When we saw MILEX™'s outstanding heat transfer resistance, pest resistance, and fire resistance — all with zero synthetic additives — we knew we were sitting on something bigger than packaging.

We answered the market's growing call for sustainable building materials by founding MILO Insulation. Our goal was simple: give homeowners and builders a green, biodegradable blow-in insulation that outperforms synthetic alternatives — while saving them thousands of dollars over a home's lifetime.

It wasn't just a product launch. It was the birth of a movement.

How We Operate

We Don't Just Sell Insulation. We Own the Entire Process.

Most insulation companies depend on third-party manufacturers, multiple distributors, and external installation teams. MILO Insulation does it differently.

From sourcing sorghum seeds from local American farmers, to manufacturing MILEX™, to delivering and installing the finished product — we manage every step. This vertical integration model means fewer delays, better quality control, and more value for our franchise owners and their customers.
