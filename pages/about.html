How It Started

It Began With a Sorghum Seed and a Big Idea.

More than 30 years ago, our founders identified something remarkable: sorghum grain could be extruded into a natural, biodegradable foam through a patented, polymer-free process. Under the MILEX Corporation banner, we developed this into an environmentally responsible packaging alternative.

The real breakthrough: MILEX was one of the most effective natural insulators on the market.

Patented Process

Polymer-free, biodegradable thermal puffs — by reimagining what a simple sorghum seed could do, our founders created MILEX® without plastics or harsh chemicals.

30+
Years of Innovation

From packaging material to the insulation of the future — three decades of real-world field experience now available to franchise owners.

The Breakthrough

From Packing Peanuts to the Insulation of the Future.

MILEX's outstanding heat transfer resistance, pest resistance, and fire resistance — all without synthetic additives — led us to found MILO Insulation.

  • Naturally high R-value and thermal performance
  • Resistant to pests and rodents without toxic treatments
  • Fire-resistant characteristics built into the material
  • Fully biodegradable and plant-based, made from sorghum
