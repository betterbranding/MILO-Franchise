Getting Started
You don't need to be an insulation expert, though prior industry experience is preferred. What matters is passion for our mission, business skills, and the drive to build. We provide all technical training.
Contact us at (800) 574-MILO or franchise@miloinsulation.com. We'll schedule a discovery call, send the FDD, and guide you through every step. There's no pressure and no obligation at this stage.
Timelines vary by market, permitting, and buildout needs, but many new owners are able to launch within 4–6 months of signing their franchise agreement, assuming they stay engaged through training and pre-opening milestones.
Training & Support
Franchise owners/managers: 14 days of comprehensive hands-on training (classroom + on-the-job). Staff (operators, installers, sales): 8 days. Our team also travels to your location for 2 days of live Grand Opening support. Ongoing training continues throughout your franchise relationship.
Manufacturing processes, sales and installation procedures, quality control, reporting, supplier contacts, marketing guidelines, and more — your complete business playbook. It's your franchise bible from day one.
Remote franchise team access, training refreshers, marketing campaign support, and operational guidance. Our support infrastructure is designed to help you build your business with confidence.
Investment & Fees
Estimated initial investment: $350,000 – $450,000, including a $75,000 franchise fee for access to our complete business model and a protected territory. Please refer to our Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD) for full details.
We don't provide direct financing, but may refer you to third-party sources on a case-by-case basis. We're happy to discuss financing options during your discovery call.
Royalty: 5% of weekly gross revenues.
Local marketing: 2% of monthly gross revenues (minimum).
Grand Opening: Minimum $15,000.
Brand fund: 1% of gross revenues.

Every dollar is clearly defined up front. No surprises.
Initial term: 10 years. Two successor options of 5 years each — up to 20 years of brand partnership. For full details, review our FDD with your advisors.
The Business Model
MILO's proprietary natural insulation made from sorghum grain — 100% biodegradable, hypoallergenic, pest-resistant, fire-resistant, and non-shrinking. It saves homeowners thousands on energy bills. Only MILO franchise owners can manufacture and install it.
You control the full chain: sourcing to manufacturing to installation. This means maximum control over quality, timeline, and margin — with no external supplier dependencies. You capture more profit at every step.
We are expanding nationwide and awarding designated territories in key markets. Contact us to check availability in your area.
