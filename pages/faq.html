Getting Started
What experience do I need to open a MILO Insulation franchise?

You don't need to be an insulation expert to be a great MILO Insulation franchise owner — though prior experience in insulation or construction is preferred. What matters most is a passion for our mission, business skills, and the drive to build something meaningful. We provide the technical training. You provide the commitment.

How do I get started?

Connect with us at (800) 574-MILO or franchise@miloinsulation.com to request franchise information. We'll schedule a discovery conversation, send you our FDD, and walk you through every step of the process together.

Training & Support
What training does MILO Insulation provide?

Your training is thorough and hands-on. Franchise owners, operating principals, and plant managers receive 14 days of comprehensive training at our MILEX Hands-On Training facility — both classroom and on-the-job. Extruder operators, installation foremen, and salespeople receive 8 days of training in the same format.

When you're ready to open, our team travels to your location for 2 days of on-site Grand Opening support. And we never truly stop training — ongoing guidance is available throughout your entire franchise relationship.

What is in the Confidential Operations Manual?

The Operations Manual is your complete business playbook. It includes proprietary manufacturing processes, sales and installation procedures, quality control standards, reporting requirements, supplier contacts, marketing guidelines, and more. It's the operational foundation of your MILO Insulation business.

What ongoing support will I receive after I open?

Our support never stops. You'll have ongoing access to our remote franchise team for questions, guidance, and troubleshooting. We provide regular training updates for you and your staff, support for marketing campaigns, and continued operational guidance. We're always just a call or click away.

Investment & Fees
How much does a MILO Insulation franchise cost?

The estimated initial investment ranges from $521,320 to $880,410, which includes a $75,000 initial franchise fee. This gives you access to our complete manufacturing, sales, and installation business model — plus a protected exclusive territory. As with any new business, having adequate capital beyond this range for early growth stages is important.

Is financing available?

We do not provide direct financing, but we may be able to refer you to third-party financing sources on a case-by-case basis.

What are the ongoing royalty and marketing fees?

Royalty Fee: 5% of weekly gross revenues

Local Marketing: 2% of monthly gross revenues (minimum)

Grand Opening Investment: Minimum $15,000

Brand Marketing Fund: 1% of gross revenues

These fees fund your continued support services, marketing programs, training updates, and system upgrades — investments in your success.

What are the terms of the franchise agreement?

The initial franchise agreement term is 10 years, with two available successor agreements of 5 years each — giving you a pathway to 20 years of operation under the MILO Insulation brand.

The Business Model
What is MILEX™ insulation?

MILEX™ is MILO Insulation's proprietary natural insulation product, made from sorghum grain through a patented extrusion process. It's 100% biodegradable, hypoallergenic, pest-resistant, fire-resistant, and non-shrinking. It delivers outstanding heat transfer resistance and can save homeowners thousands of dollars over the lifetime of their home.

Only MILO Insulation franchise owners can manufacture and install MILEX™ — giving you a truly exclusive product in your territory.

What does "vertical integration" mean for my franchise?

It means you own the entire value chain — from sourcing sorghum seeds from local farmers, to manufacturing MILEX™ at your plant, to delivering and installing it for end customers. This gives you maximum control over quality, timeline, and margin — without depending on outside suppliers or distributors.

What territories are available?

MILO Insulation is currently targeting Texas and Oklahoma for our initial franchise expansion, with plans to grow into the Midwest and beyond. Available territories are limited and protected — meaning once your territory is yours, no other MILO franchise can compete within it.

Contact us to learn which territories are currently open.
