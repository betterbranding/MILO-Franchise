Why Temple & Waco

Two Fast-Growing Cities. One Massive Opportunity.

Temple and Waco sit at the heart of Central Texas's explosive growth corridor. Fort Cavazos (formerly Fort Hood) drives steady housing demand, while Waco's renaissance — led by a booming tourism economy and Baylor University — has made it one of Texas's hottest residential markets.

New subdivisions are going up at a pace that outstrips the trades available to serve them. Builders need reliable, high-performance insulation partners — and homeowners increasingly want sustainable options. MILO fills both gaps.

  • I-35 corridor — one of the top growth corridors in the entire state
  • Fort Cavazos military base drives consistent housing turnover and new builds
  • Baylor University and Waco's revitalization attracting young professionals
  • Texas-friendly business climate with no state income tax
  • Strong builder and contractor networks hungry for differentiated products
