Home Inspection Walkthrough

🏠 The MILO Inspection Process

Every sale starts with a thorough home inspection. This is your opportunity to identify insulation problems, build trust with the homeowner, and demonstrate the value of MILEX insulation.

  1. Arrival & Introduction
    Arrive on time in MILO branded attire. Introduce yourself, explain the inspection process, and set expectations for the visit (30–60 minutes).
  2. Exterior Assessment
    Walk the exterior of the home. Note roof condition, siding gaps, foundation vents, and any visible insulation issues. Take photos.
  3. Attic Inspection
    Access the attic (with homeowner permission). Check existing insulation type, depth, and condition. Note air leaks, bypasses, and ventilation.
  4. Thermal Camera Scan
    Use your FLIR thermal camera to scan walls, ceilings, and floors. Show homeowner the thermal images — hot/cold spots reveal insulation gaps.
  5. Crawl Space / Basement Check
    If accessible, inspect crawl space or basement for moisture, air infiltration, and insulation condition at rim joists and band boards.
  6. Findings Presentation
    Sit down with the homeowner. Walk through your findings using photos and thermal images. Explain each problem area and the MILO solution.

Using Your Thermal Camera

📷 Thermal Imaging for Energy Audits

Your thermal imaging camera is your most powerful visual sales tool. It reveals invisible insulation problems that homeowners can see and understand immediately.

Key areas to scan:

  • Exterior walls (inside and outside) — reveals missing or compressed insulation
  • Ceiling/attic boundary — shows heat escaping through gaps
  • Around windows and doors — identifies air leaks and poor sealing
  • Electrical outlets on exterior walls — common air infiltration points
  • Rim joists and band boards — often uninsulated or poorly insulated
💡 Pro Tip: Schedule inspections for early morning or on days with a significant temperature difference between inside and outside. This creates maximum thermal contrast in your images and makes problems more obvious to homeowners.

Identifying Selling Points

🎯 Common Insulation Problems = Sales Opportunities

  • High energy bills: "Your energy bills are higher than they need to be because heat is escaping through these gaps we identified."
  • Uncomfortable rooms: "The reason some rooms are hotter/colder than others is uneven insulation coverage."
  • Drafts and air leaks: "These thermal images show exactly where air is infiltrating your home."
  • Old/deteriorated insulation: "Your existing insulation has settled and degraded — it's performing far below its original R-value."
  • Fire safety: "Unlike traditional insulation, MILEX is self-extinguishing — let me show you our fire test demonstration."
  • Moisture/mold concerns: "Proper insulation and air sealing prevents condensation that leads to mold growth."

Closing Techniques

🤝 Effective Closing Strategies

  1. The Assumptive Close
    "Based on what we found, I'd recommend starting with the attic and rim joists. When would be a good day for us to get started?"
  2. The Fire Test Close
    Perform the fire test demonstration. Let the visual impact speak for itself. "As you can see, MILEX insulation doesn't burn — that's the difference."
  3. The Energy Savings Close
    "Most homeowners see a 20-40% reduction in energy costs. That means this project could pay for itself in X years."
  4. The Urgency Close
    "We have availability next week, and material costs are going up. Let's lock in your pricing today."
  5. The Comfort Close
    "Imagine finally having every room in your home be the same comfortable temperature — that's what proper insulation does."
