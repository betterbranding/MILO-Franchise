Brand Guidelines

🎨 MILO Brand Standards

Maintaining a consistent brand across all franchise locations is essential. Follow these guidelines for all marketing materials:

  • Logo Usage: Always use the official MILO Insulation logo. Do not modify colors, proportions, or add effects.
  • Colors: Primary Navy (#1B2A4A), Accent Orange (#E87722), White, Light Gray backgrounds.
  • Fonts: Montserrat for headings, Open Sans for body text. Maintain these in all digital and print materials.
  • Imagery: Use professional photos of real installations, happy homeowners, and your team in branded attire.
  • Tone of Voice: Professional, knowledgeable, friendly. Position as trusted local experts.
📁 Brand Assets: Official logos, brand colors, templates, and guidelines are available in the Supply Hub. Contact Better Branding for custom print materials.

Social Media Setup

📱 Social Media Account Checklist

Facebook Business Page
Create "MILO Insulation of [Your City]" business page. Complete all profile sections, add logo, cover photo, services, and contact info. Enable messaging and reviews.
Instagram Business Account
Set up @miloinsulation_[yourcity] Instagram business account. Link to Facebook page. Start posting before/after project photos.
Google Business Profile
Claim and verify your Google Business Profile. Critical for local search visibility. Add photos, services, business hours, and service area.
📝 Content Tips: Post 3–5 times per week. Mix content: before/after photos (40%), educational tips (30%), team/behind-the-scenes (20%), promotions (10%). Fire test demonstration videos perform extremely well on social media.

Local SEO Setup

🔍 Get Found Locally

Local SEO helps homeowners in your territory find you when they search for insulation services.

  1. Optimize Google Business Profile
    Complete every section: business description, services, photos, Q&A. Post weekly updates.
  2. Get listed in online directories
    Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor, BBB, and local directories. Ensure NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency.
  3. Collect Google Reviews
    Ask every satisfied customer for a Google review. Respond to all reviews — positive and negative.
  4. Create local content
    If you have a website, publish local content: "[City] insulation guide," "energy saving tips for [City] homeowners."

Review Generation Strategy

⭐ Building Your Online Reputation

Reviews are your most powerful trust builder. Aim for 1 review per completed job.

  1. Ask at the right moment
    Request the review during the post-job walkthrough when the customer is most satisfied. "If you're happy with the work, a Google review would mean the world to us."
  2. Make it easy
    Send a direct link to your Google review page via text. One tap and they're there.
  3. Follow up
    If they don't leave a review within 48 hours, send a friendly reminder via email.
  4. Respond to every review
    Thank reviewers by name. For negative reviews, respond professionally and offer to resolve the issue offline.

Referral Program

🤝 Customer Referral Program

Word-of-mouth referrals are the highest-converting leads. Set up a simple referral incentive:

  • Referral reward: Offer a gift card, discount on future services, or cash incentive for each referral that books
  • Referral cards: Give customers physical referral cards to hand out to friends and neighbors
  • Track referrals: Log referral sources in JobNimbus to track which customers send the most business
  • Thank your referrers: Send a thank-you note or gift when their referral converts
