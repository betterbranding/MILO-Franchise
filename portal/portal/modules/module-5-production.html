MILEX Insulation Product Overview

🧱 About MILEX Insulation

MILEX is MILO's proprietary insulation product line, offering both spray foam and blown-in insulation solutions. MILEX products are engineered for superior thermal performance, fire resistance, and ease of application.

Key Product Features:

  • Superior R-value per inch compared to traditional insulation
  • Self-extinguishing fire-resistant properties
  • Excellent air sealing and moisture barrier capabilities
  • Long lifespan — does not settle, sag, or degrade over time
  • Eco-friendly formulation with low VOC emissions

Product Applications:

  • Spray Foam: Wall cavities, rim joists, crawl spaces, attic rooflines
  • Blown-In: Open attic floors, enclosed wall cavities, hard-to-reach areas

📋 Spray Foam Application Steps

  1. Pre-Job Setup
    Inspect the work area, protect surfaces, ensure ventilation, and set up the MX-750 Extruder per manufacturer specifications.
  2. Material Preparation
    Load spray foam materials, check mix ratios, and test equipment operation before beginning application.
  3. Application Technique
    Apply spray foam in even passes, maintaining consistent distance and speed. Build up in layers rather than single thick passes.
  4. Thickness Verification
    Use depth gauges to verify proper thickness and R-value at multiple points throughout the application area.
  5. Cleanup & Inspection
    Clean equipment immediately after use. Inspect the finished application for voids, thin spots, or over-spray. Touch up as needed.

📋 Blown-In Application Steps

  1. Area Assessment
    Measure the area, calculate material needed, and identify any obstructions or special considerations (wiring, plumbing, etc.).
  2. Machine Setup
    Set up the MILEX Blower & Vacuum, load insulation material, and calibrate feed rate for optimal density.
  3. Application
    Apply blown-in insulation evenly across the area, working from far end to exit point. Maintain consistent depth throughout.
  4. Depth & Density Check
    Use depth rulers to verify consistent coverage. Check density in multiple spots to ensure proper R-value.
  5. Final Inspection
    Verify coverage around obstacles, ensure baffles are properly maintained at soffit vents, and clean up the work area.

Fire Test Station — Build & Procedure

🔥 Fire Test Demonstration Station

The Fire Test Station is one of your most powerful sales tools. It demonstrates the self-extinguishing fire-resistant properties of MILEX insulation in a live, side-by-side comparison with competitor products.

Build Instructions

  1. Assemble the metal frame
    Build a portable, fire-resistant frame that holds two insulation samples side by side. Use metal or fire-rated materials only.
  2. Prepare sample panels
    Cut MILEX insulation and a competitor product into identical-sized demonstration panels. Mount securely in the frame.
  3. Set up safety equipment
    Position fire extinguisher, safety mat, and ensure you're in a well-ventilated, controlled environment.
  4. Practice the demonstration
    Run through the demonstration multiple times before performing for customers. Perfect your timing and talking points.

Testing Procedure

Apply a controlled flame (butane torch) to both the competitor sample and the MILEX sample. The MILEX insulation will demonstrate self-extinguishing properties — the flame goes out on its own when the heat source is removed. The competitor product will typically continue to burn or melt. Document all results with video for use in sales presentations and marketing.

Safety Protocols & PPE Requirements

🦺 Required Personal Protective Equipment

Respirator / Dust Mask
N95 or P100 respirator required during all insulation application. Full-face respirator recommended for spray foam.
Safety Glasses / Goggles
ANSI Z87.1 rated. Sealed goggles for spray foam; safety glasses for blown-in.
Protective Coveralls
Disposable Tyvek coveralls for spray foam work. Protects skin and clothing from overspray.
Work Gloves
Chemical-resistant gloves for spray foam; standard work gloves for blown-in applications.
Knee Pads
Required for crawl space work and attic applications.
Hearing Protection
Earplugs or earmuffs when operating blower equipment or extruder.

Quality Control Checklist

✅ Post-Application Quality Checks

Coverage verification
Verify insulation covers all specified areas with no gaps or voids.
Thickness / depth measurements
Measure and document insulation depth at multiple points to confirm R-value requirements are met.
Thermal imaging scan
Use thermal camera to scan for cold spots, gaps, or thermal bridging after application.
Before & after photos
Document the job with before and after photos for customer records and portfolio.
Customer walkthrough
Walk the customer through the completed work, explain what was done, and answer any questions.
