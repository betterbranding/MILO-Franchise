Our Mission & Brand Story

🏠 Who We Are

MILO Insulation is a franchise system specializing in spray foam and blown-in insulation solutions for residential and commercial properties. Our proprietary MILEX insulation products deliver superior energy efficiency, fire resistance, and long-term value to homeowners.

We believe every home deserves proper insulation — and every franchise owner deserves a proven business model that generates real results. Our mission is to make professional insulation services accessible in every market through empowered local franchise operators.

What to Expect During Onboarding

📋 Your 9-Module Onboarding Journey

Your onboarding consists of 9 sequential modules. Complete each one to unlock the next. Here's an overview:

Module 1 — Welcome to MILO

You are here! Get oriented and understand the journey ahead.

Module 2 — Legal & Business Setup

Form your business entity, secure insurance, and handle licensing.

Module 3 — Contacts & Vendors

Meet your approved vendors and set up supplier accounts.

Module 4 — Equipment Ordering

Order your sales, inspection, and production equipment.

Module 5 — Production Training

Learn MILEX insulation products and application techniques.

Module 6 — Sales Process

Master the home inspection and sales workflow.

Module 7 — CRM & Operations

Set up JobNimbus and learn your operational workflows.

Module 8 — Marketing Setup

Build your local marketing presence and brand assets.

Module 9 — Launch Checklist

Final review, goal setting, and launch!

⏱ Timeline Expectation: Plan to complete your full onboarding in 2–4 weeks. Stay on pace by completing at least 2–3 modules per week. Your training coordinator will check in weekly to track progress.
