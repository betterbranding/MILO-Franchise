JobNimbus Setup

💻 Getting Started with JobNimbus

JobNimbus is your Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and job management platform. It's where you'll manage leads, schedule jobs, create invoices, and track every customer interaction.

  1. Receive your login credentials
    MILO corporate will provision your JobNimbus account. Check your email for login credentials and setup instructions.
  2. Complete your company profile
    Add your business name, logo, contact information, and service area to your JobNimbus profile.
  3. Download the mobile app
    Install the JobNimbus app on your phone and tablet for field access. Available on iOS and Android.
  4. Configure notifications
    Set up email and push notifications for new leads, appointments, and task reminders.
  5. Add team members
    As you hire, add team members with appropriate access levels (Admin, Sales, Production).

Lead Management

📞 Lead Response Workflow

Speed to lead is critical. Your response time directly impacts your close rate.

⚡ The 5-Minute Rule: Contact every new lead within 5 minutes of receiving it. Studies show that leads contacted within 5 minutes are 21x more likely to convert than leads contacted after 30 minutes.
  1. Lead comes in
    Via phone call, website form, GHL funnel, or referral. Automatically logged in JobNimbus.
  2. Immediate response
    Call or text within 5 minutes. Introduce yourself and MILO. Ask about their insulation concerns.
  3. Qualify the lead
    Determine: homeowner vs renter, property type, age of home, current insulation issues, timeline, and budget.
  4. Book the inspection
    Schedule a free home inspection. Offer the next available slot. Send calendar invite and confirmation.

Job Scheduling & Calendar

📅 Calendar Management

Use JobNimbus's built-in calendar to schedule inspections, production days, and follow-ups. Best practices:

  • Block morning hours (8–12) for inspections — homeowners prefer mornings
  • Schedule production jobs in full-day or half-day blocks
  • Reserve 30 minutes between appointments for travel and prep
  • Set recurring time blocks for follow-up calls (e.g., 2–3 PM daily)
  • Sync JobNimbus calendar with Google Calendar for cross-platform access

Invoicing & Payments

💳 Getting Paid

JobNimbus handles invoicing and can integrate with payment processors for seamless collection.

  • Deposits: Collect a deposit (typically 50%) at contract signing
  • Progress payments: For larger jobs, set milestone payment schedules
  • Final payment: Collect remaining balance upon job completion and customer sign-off
  • Payment methods: Accept credit cards, ACH, checks, and digital payments
  • Financing: Offer financing options through MILO's approved lending partners

Communication Tracking

💬 Keep Everything in One Place

Log every customer interaction in JobNimbus — calls, texts, emails, and notes. This creates a complete history and ensures nothing falls through the cracks.

  • Log all phone calls with date, duration, and summary notes
  • Track email communications via JobNimbus email integration
  • Store photos, documents, and proposals in the customer record
  • Use task assignments to delegate follow-ups to team members
