Building Supplies & Materials

🏠

Home Depot PRO

Building supplies, construction materials, tools, and hardware for jobs and operations.
  • Set up a Home Depot PRO account for commercial/contractor pricing
  • Register your business with tax-exempt status (if applicable)
  • Download the Home Depot PRO app for mobile ordering
🔧

Lowe's PRO

Building supplies, materials, and tools — secondary supplier for competitive pricing and availability.
  • Set up a Lowe's PRO account for volume pricing
  • Register your business for Lowe's PRO loyalty rewards
  • Explore Lowe's PRO supply delivery options in your territory
📦

Amazon Business

Miscellaneous supplies, equipment, office items, and hard-to-find specialty products.
  • Set up an Amazon Business account with your business entity
  • Enable tax-exempt purchasing (if applicable)
  • Set up approval workflows for team purchases

Equipment Vendors

⚙️

AFCAT USA

MILEX Blower & Vacuum equipment — essential machinery for blown-in insulation applications.
  • Contact AFCAT USA to place your MILEX Blower & Vacuum order
  • Confirm equipment specifications with MILO corporate
  • Schedule delivery and coordinate installation/training
🔩

Maddox Metal Works

MX-750 Extruder — primary production equipment for spray foam insulation application.
  • Contact Maddox Metal Works to order your MX-750 Extruder
  • Confirm model and configuration with MILO Supply Chain team
  • Arrange delivery timeline and review maintenance schedule

Insulation Materials

🧱

Gemini Insulation Supply

Insulation materials supplier — spray foam, blown-in insulation material, and related accessories.
  • Set up a wholesale supply account with Gemini Insulation Supply
  • Review the product catalog and pricing tiers
  • Establish a recurring order schedule for materials
  • Confirm delivery logistics for your territory

Branding & Apparel

Fierce Threads

MILO branded apparel — shirts, polos, hats, jackets, and other branded clothing for your team.
  • Contact Fierce Threads to place your initial apparel order

  • Review size guide and place bulk orders for future crew members

Better Branding

Print materials, marketing collateral, business cards, door hangers, flyers, and signage.
  • Set up your account with Better Branding

  • Discuss vehicle wrap options and pricing

Local Suppliers (Territory-Specific)
