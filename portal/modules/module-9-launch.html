Goal Setting

  • Complete 10+ home inspections
  • Close your first 2–3 jobs
  • Generate $5,000–$15,000 in revenue
  • Collect your first 3 Google reviews
  • Post on social media at least 15 times
  • Build a pipeline of 15+ active leads
  • Complete 1 fire test demonstration for a potential customer

📅 60-Day Goals

  • Complete 25+ cumulative home inspections
  • Close 8–10 total jobs
  • Generate $15,000–$35,000 cumulative revenue
  • Achieve a 40%+ close rate on inspections
  • Collect 8+ Google reviews (maintain 4.5+ star average)
  • Establish 2–3 referral sources (realtors, contractors, etc.)
  • Hire your first crew member (if applicable)

📅 90-Day Goals

  • Complete 50+ cumulative home inspections
  • Close 18–25 total jobs
  • Generate $35,000–$75,000 cumulative revenue
  • Develop a consistent weekly lead flow (5+ leads/week)
  • Collect 15+ Google reviews
  • Establish a regular production schedule (3+ jobs/week)
  • Identify your top marketing channels by ROI
  • Begin training a crew for production efficiency

First Customer Acquisition

🏁 Getting Your First Customers

Your first few customers are critical. Here's a proven strategy to get started:

  • Friends & Family: Offer free inspections to friends, family, and neighbors. Even if they don't buy, they'll refer you.
  • Door Knocking: Canvas neighborhoods with older homes (20+ years). Bring door hangers and offer free energy audits.
  • Social Media Announcement: Announce your business on personal and business social media. Ask friends to share.
  • Nextdoor: Post on Nextdoor as a local business. Offer a grand opening special.
  • Realtor Partnerships: Connect with local realtors who need home inspection insights for buyers/sellers.
  • Home Shows & Events: Set up a booth at local home shows with your fire test station — it draws crowds.

Launch Promotion Ideas

🎉 Grand Opening & Launch Promotions

  • "First 10 Customers" Special: Offer a discount or bonus service to your first 10 customers to build momentum and reviews.
  • Free Energy Audit Month: Offer free thermal imaging inspections for your first month. Convert to paid jobs.
  • Neighborhood Blitz: Pick a target neighborhood, canvas with door hangers, and offer group pricing for multiple homes.
  • Social Media Contest: "Share this post for a chance to win a free insulation inspection" — builds awareness.
  • Partner Cross-Promotions: Partner with local HVAC companies, roofers, or realtors for mutual referrals.
