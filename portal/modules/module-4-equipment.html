Sales & Inspection Equipment

🔍 Field Equipment Checklist

This is the equipment you'll need for home inspections, energy audits, and sales appointments:

Thermal Imaging Camera (FLIR or equivalent)
Essential for energy audits and identifying insulation gaps. Used to visually show homeowners heat loss and air infiltration. FLIR C5 or E8 recommended.
Telescopic Ladder
Compact, portable ladder for attic access and roof inspections. Telescopic models are easy to transport in your vehicle.
Knee Pads
Professional-grade knee pads for crawl space inspections and attic work. Look for gel or foam padding.
Dig Rod / Soil Probe
Used for checking wall cavity depth and measuring existing insulation levels. Stainless steel preferred.
High-Lumen Flashlight
Bright, reliable flashlight for attic, crawl space, and dark area inspections. Minimum 1000 lumens recommended.
MILO Branded Hats
Order from Fierce Threads. Professional branded hats for field work and customer-facing appointments.
MILO Branded Shirts / Polos
Order from Fierce Threads. Branded shirts and polos for your team. Order multiple sizes for crew members.
Safety Glasses
ANSI-rated safety glasses for inspections and production work. Impact-resistant, anti-fog lenses preferred.
Measuring Tape
25-foot minimum. Used for measuring rooms, attic spaces, and wall cavities for accurate quoting.
Clipboard / Tablet for Inspections
For recording inspection findings, taking photos, and presenting proposals. A rugged tablet (iPad or similar) is recommended for digital workflows.

Fire Test Station Build

🔥 Fire Test Demonstration Station

Every MILO franchisee builds a Fire Test Station — a portable demonstration setup that showcases the fire-resistant properties of MILEX insulation to potential customers. This is one of your most powerful sales tools.

What You'll Need

  • 🔧 Metal frame or stand (fire-resistant base)
  • 🔧 MILEX insulation sample panels
  • 🔧 Competitor insulation sample panels (for comparison)
  • 🔧 Butane torch or controlled flame source
  • 🔧 Fire extinguisher (safety requirement)
  • 🔧 Safety mat / fire-resistant surface
  • 🔧 Timer / stopwatch for documentation
  • 🔧 Camera / phone for recording demonstrations

📋 Build Diagram Reference

Detailed build diagrams and assembly instructions will be provided during your production training session (Module 5). Your trainer will walk through the complete build process.

🧪 Testing Procedure Overview

  1. Prepare the station
    Set up the fire test station on a fire-resistant surface in a well-ventilated area. Have the fire extinguisher within arm's reach.
  2. Position insulation samples
    Place MILEX insulation sample and competitor sample side by side for direct comparison.
  3. Apply flame to samples
    Apply the controlled flame to each sample for the same duration. Start with the competitor sample.
  4. Observe self-extinguishing properties
    MILEX insulation will demonstrate self-extinguishing properties — the flame goes out on its own when the heat source is removed. Document the difference.
  5. Document results
    Record the demonstration on video. Capture the homeowner's reaction. This content can be used in your marketing with permission.
